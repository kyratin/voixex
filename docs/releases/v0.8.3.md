Voixex 0.8.3 excludes Voixex playback from screen-share audio so channel voices and app sounds are not sent back through the stream.

- Desktop capture requests own-application audio exclusion while preserving local playback. Electron is pinned to 43.8.7, which includes the native exclusion fix missing from the previous runtime.
- Captured audio must confirm exclusion and remain live. Unsupported, denied or failed audio capture falls back to video only; unrestricted system-loopback audio is never used as a fallback.
- Android 10+ playback capture excludes Voixex's UID and app playback. The auxiliary publisher does not subscribe to the call or open a second physical microphone. Screen audio is published separately from voice and released when sharing stops.
- Retains 0.8.2 camera speaking indicators and 0.8.1 voice recovery.

Windows system-audio exclusion requires OS build 20348 or newer; older Windows systems share video only. Android can capture only audio that other applications allow to be captured. OS recording permission is still required. Automated checks do not certify every physical Windows/Android device.

Android APK, macOS Apple Silicon DMG and Windows x64 installer are attached. Server runtime and Docker Hub image remain 0.8.0.
